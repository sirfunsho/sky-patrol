How to play & controls

Keyboard

  • ←→↑↓ or WASD — fly
  • Weapons fire on their own
  • P or Esc — pause
  • M — mute sound

Touch

  • Touch anywhere: a stick appears under your thumb
  • Push it to fly — the aircraft stays clear of your hand
  • Tap the ❙❙ button to pause
  • Big buttons for start, resume and replay

Items

  • Spread a widening fan of bolts
  • Rapid a faster stream of shots
  • Laser a piercing beam
  • Shield soaks one hit
  • Repair one hull point
  • EMP clears bullets, hurts everything
  • Star bonus points

Pulse is your default weapon and never runs out. Every weapon pod lasts 18 seconds — take the same one again to make it stronger, up to level 3. When a weapon expires you drop back to Pulse. Weapons and upgrades only last for the run you are flying.
